# Correct popup headwords, readings and conjugation rules

Both editions now show only real Japanese headwords and readings in popups.
Each one still finds all **9,152 source records across 13 sources**. Both have
**7,640 term-bank rows**.

- **Readings:** a reading is kept only when it is an exact kana spelling of its
  headword. Garbage readings no longer appear as furigana or match hovered text
  — notation (`と～ない`), joined alternatives (`かなかなあ`,
  `ずにはいられないないではいられない`), truncated scraper output (`なんのこ`,
  `にまっている`) or another construction's reading (`にむけて` for `に向け`).
  Notation headwords such as `AながらB` no longer appear for `ながら`.
- **Synthetic lemmas:** 絵でわかる日本語, どんなとき and 毎日のんびり日本語教師 filed
  some negative constructions under affirmative headwords nobody writes
  (`ずにはいる`, `もする`, `ねばなる`, `ないわけにはいく`). These records now appear
  under their real forms, such as `ずにはいられない`. Hovering
  `ずにはいられなかった` no longer reports a “passive” of `はいる`.
- **Producer lookup keys:** a kana headword is also found by the form its
  producer looked it up by: `たかがく` under `たかが`, `わけ` under
  `わけにはいかない`.
- **Typos and corrupted headwords:** どんなとき's `と言いえ` is found under `とは言え`
  and `とはいえ`. NINJAL's `と言っても過言ではない` was cut to `と言ではない` by
  broken ruby markup in the source XML. It is now found by its full form.
- A source that files one article twice shows it once in that row.
- Every earlier headword and article address still works. Headwords kept only
  for compatibility cannot be reached by conjugation or by reading.

Update or reimport the dictionary in Yomitan, Hachidori or another popup app.
Try `笑わずにはいられなかった。` at `ずには`, or `学生とはいえ、` at `とはいえ`.

Both ZIPs were imported into the real Yomitan importer/translator and
Hachidori's hoshidicts engine. They were hovered at 12,177 example highlights
from six sources. Hits showing a reading that is not a spelling of the headword
fell from 7,576 to 0. Hits on synthetic headwords fell from 160 to 0. Source
findability was unchanged. Schemas, all article links, old routes and SHA-256
checksums were checked as before.
