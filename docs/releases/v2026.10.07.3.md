# Readings for kanji headwords

Kanji headwords that no source gave a reading for now have one, so popups show
furigana for them and kana-written text finds them. Before this release, 658
lookup headwords lacked a reading. **617 now have one**, and every reading is an
exact kana spelling of its headword. The other 41 are left without on purpose
(see below).

- Hover `そのうえ` and get `その上`; `いつのまにか` gets `いつの間にか`, and
  `てちょうだい` gets `て頂戴`. Grammar terms such as `連用形`, `ク活用` and
  `上二段活用` (かみにだんかつよう) now show furigana.
- Counters and affixes get the reading they take in their lesson: `頭`→とう,
  `本`→ほん, `足`→そく, `的`→てき, `滴`→てき, `人`→にん. IMABI prints many of
  these itself (`～棟（むね・トウ）`, `～滴てき`).
- `ヶ月`, `ヵ所`, `各々`, `所以`, `就中` and `当って` are now recognised by the
  reading check, which had rejected their correct readings.

Readings were chosen from four kinds of evidence:

1. a reading the source prints beside the headword (24 headwords);
2. JMdict's reading of the whole headword (425). When JMdict lists several, the one
   the headword's own example sentences use (`歳`: さい 25 times, とし 8);
3. agreement of Sudachi, Jiten and token-by-token JMdict readings, or the
   headword's own sentences (129);
4. 39 hand-reviewed decisions where the evidence disagreed or was wrong.

No single tool was enough. Sudachi alone reads `いつの間にか` as いつのあいだにか
and `か年` as かとし, which JMdict corrects. Review fixed `時間直示`, which Sudachi
read as じかんなおじ, and `的`, where the dictionaries prefer the noun reading
まと. The lessons' own sentences settled counters such as `頭` (とう, not あたま).

**Left without a reading (41).** Popups also match hovered text by reading, so
these would appear in the wrong places:

- 29 counters and affixes whose reading spells a common particle or auxiliary,
  such as `化`/`課` (か), `羽` (わ), `余` (よ), `増す` (ます), `枚` (まい) and
  `代`/`台`/`第` (だい);
- 8 one-mora prefixes and counters that would match the first kana of other
  words, such as `個`/`戸` (こ in これ), `無` (む) and `非` (ひ);
- 3 verbs whose inflections spell common words: `逸れる` (それ), `渡す` (わたし)
  and `立つ` (たって);
- one typo row. Rows kept only for old links also have no reading.

Both editions were imported into the real Yomitan importer and translator and
into Hachidori's hoshidicts engine. In both, hovering the kana spelling of each
of the 617 readings finds its kanji headword. Across 15,014 hover positions in
the sources' example sentences, the new readings add 353 matches; most are kana
spellings of the same verbs, such as `にくる` for `に来る`. `make verify-popup`
also checks that `か` and `わ` do not bring up `化` or `羽`.

Readings use JMdict (EDRDG, CC BY-SA 4.0), SudachiDict-full and the open-source
Jiten parser. Their versions and the Jiten responses are recorded with the
committed catalog. Builds stay offline.

- `bees-ultimate-grammar-dictionary.zip`: original source edition.
- `bees-ultimate-grammar-dictionary-en.zip`: English edition.
- `SHA256SUMS`: archive checksums.

See [coverage, examples and verification details](https://github.com/bee-san/bees-ultimate-grammar-dictionary/blob/main/docs/lookup-coverage.md).
