# くらべてわかる日本語表現文型辞典 joins the dictionary

The 14th source is **くらべてわかる日本語表現文型辞典** (Jリサーチ出版, 2008), from a
reviewed Yomitan conversion (revision 2026.10.20.2) whose producer checked all
363 patterns against page images of the book. Its strength is comparison: for
each sense it sets the pattern against similar expressions and shows which
sentences work (○) and which do not (×).

- **363 patterns, 1,070 lookup spellings.** Each pattern is one record; its other
  spellings (`あげくに`, `挙げ句`, `揚げ句` …) are lookup forms of it.
- **Everything in the book's entries is kept**: every sense (意味), all
  2,856 examples (例文), usage cautions (注意), every contrast (対比) with its
  ○/× sentence pairs, and the connection rules (接続). Stacked alternatives keep
  their brackets, e.g. `給料｛○からいうと／×からすると｝社長と同額だ`. The book's original
  connection notation is kept in provenance. The 14 diagrams (in 7 patterns) are
  not packaged; the prose around them is.
- **222 patterns join existing entries** where the match is unambiguous, so
  `あげく`, `や否や` or `にそって` now include its explanation beside the other sources.
  The rest are new headwords or appear as their own section on an existing page.
- **English edition:** its explanations and 2,856 example sentences are
  translated with GPT-6 Luna and labelled as translations, like the other
  monolingual sources. Each ○/× sentence keeps its Japanese, followed by
  its translation, because the judgement is about the Japanese wording.

## Existing entries are unchanged

The new source is matched against the finished alignment of the other 13 sources
and never changes it. Every previously published headword and article URL is kept,
and no existing explanation moves to another page. Five pages that only pointed
elsewhere (`たで`, `としたところで`, `なしで`, `にあたらない`, `向きの`) now show this
source's explanation of that exact form.

Both editions now have **7,680 lookup entries**, and popup lookup covers all
**9,515 source records** across 14 sources.

The book's data is shared for non-commercial learning use only.
