# 絵でわかる日本語, now with its pictures

絵でわかる日本語 explains grammar through illustrations, but until now this
library used its text-only edition. This release reads Salwynn's illustrated
edition (`edewakaru_v6 2024-11-30`, listed in MarvNC's yomitan-dictionaries
collection) beside the text banks, and adds each post's picture.

- **In Yomitan**, open the 絵でわかる日本語 section of a card to see the post's
  picture, sized to the popup.
- **On the website**, every 絵でわかる日本語 explanation shows its picture at the
  top of its section, like the original blog posts: 955 pictures.
- **153 picture-only posts** from the producer's 自動詞・他動詞 series (`割る` and
  `割れる`, `倒す` and `倒れる`, `見る` and `見せる`…) are new. Hovering those verbs
  now shows their picture. On the website, 202 new pages hold only these
  pictures, and 51 existing pages, such as `上げる`, `出す` and `入る`, gain one as
  an extra section.

The website shows the producer's original pictures. The dictionaries carry
AVIF copies at quality 40: 99.0 MB of pictures become 14.2 MB, with the
furigana inside them still legible, so each archive grows by about 14.4 MB
(original edition 17.6 → 32.0 MB, English 18.6 → 33.1 MB).

Two readings in the picture-only posts contradicted their own pictures (`倒す`
read こかす, `回る` read めぐる). They are corrected to たおす and まわる, from the
furigana printed in the pictures.

Every previously published headword and article URL is kept. No existing
explanation moved: the new posts join the finished alignment without changing it.

- `bees-ultimate-grammar-dictionary.zip`: original source edition.
- `bees-ultimate-grammar-dictionary-en.zip`: English edition.
- `SHA256SUMS`: archive checksums.
